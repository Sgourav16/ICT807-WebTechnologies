What the table can tell you

It helps compare current employment size, recent growth, work patterns, earnings bands, skill levels and projected employment change using a common structure.

What it cannot tell you

It cannot determine personal suitability, guarantee a salary, predict vacancies in a particular suburb, or measure the number of applicants competing for a role.
