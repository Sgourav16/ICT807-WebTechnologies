Labour market insights

Turn individual occupation records into a few clear patterns while keeping the limitations of labour-market data visible.

Data interpretationPatterns, not predictions

Projected change

Selected occupations with strong 2030 outlook

The bars compare projected percentage change, not the number of future job openings.

Employment size matters

A smaller growth rate in a very large occupation can still represent more additional jobs than a high growth rate in a small occupation.

Unemployment categories need context

An occupation can experience skill shortages in particular areas even when its unemployment rate is relatively high.

National data can hide local variation

Employment prospects can differ substantially across states, territories and regions, so national indicators should not be treated as local guarantees.
