CareerCompass AU / Data & Methodology

Data & methodology

Understand where CareerCompass AU gets its information, what each measure means, and the limits that should guide responsible interpretation.

Primary datasetAustralian Jobs 2026

Dataset source

CareerCompass AU uses the Australian Jobs 2026 – Occupation Matrix, published by Jobs and Skills Australia. The Matrix provides summary statistical information for around 330 occupations grouped into 21 broad fields of work.

Open official source ↗

How the website integrates the data

1. Official CSV

The source dataset is retained in CSV format to preserve provenance and keep the project easy to inspect.

2. Browser loads the file

A small JavaScript function reads the CSV when a data-driven page opens.

3. Values are interpreted

Missing values are treated as unavailable rather than zero, and occupation indicators are converted into clear display labels.

4. HTML updates dynamically

Search results, comparison tables and summary views are created from the loaded records.

Measure definitions

Employment
Total people employed in the occupation at November 2025, including full-time and part-time workers.
3-year employment change
Change in employment levels and percentage change over the three years to November 2025.
Working part-time
Share of workers working fewer than 35 hours per week.
Female / aged 15–24
Share of people employed in the occupation who are female or aged 15 to 24 respectively.
Unemployment rate
Presented as below average, average or above average relative to the 2025 occupation average.
Median earnings
Indicative weekly earnings bands before tax for full-time workers. Exact personal earnings cannot be inferred.
Skill level
Five levels describing the range and complexity of occupational tasks and typical qualification equivalence.
Projected employment change
Percentage change in employment projected over the five years to May 2030.

Key limitations

  • Labour-market conditions can change after publication.
  • National figures may not represent a particular local labour market.
  • Projected growth does not show the number of applicants competing for jobs.
  • Earnings bands are indicative and cannot predict an individual's pay.
  • Some occupations have unavailable values, which CareerCompass displays as “Not available”.
  • Career decisions should also consider interests, aptitude, working conditions, goals and current training requirements.

Dataset facts

  • Publisher: Jobs and Skills Australia
  • Publication: 30 June 2026
  • Format: CSV
  • Approx. 330 occupations
  • 21 career groupings
  • Employment reference: Nov 2025
  • Projection horizon: May 2030
Responsible-use principle: CareerCompass AU presents the Matrix as background evidence. It deliberately avoids language suggesting that any occupation guarantees employment, salary or personal suitability.
